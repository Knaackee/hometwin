# PV module dimensions

The editor offers representative physical footprints, not a universal module standard.
Dimensions are stored in metres as `width` and `length` before rotation; `portrait: false`
swaps the rendered axes. Legacy plans retain 1.13 × 1.72 m unless explicitly edited.
Changing a format or orientation affects the current field/row/module selection without
repacking its positions. New rows use the chosen dimensions and orientation.
Overlaps and the 10 cm roof-edge guideline are advisory, not installation certification.

Presets (length × width, mm), verified 2026-10-18:

- 1650 × 992: [Jinko installation manual](https://jinkosolar.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Installation-Manual-16.10.23.pdf).
- 1722 × 1134: [Jinko product catalogue](https://www.jinkosolar.com/uploads/2201212.pdf).
- 1762 × 1134 and 1961 × 1134: [Trina rooftop portfolio](https://vertexsplus.trinasolar.com/de/).
- 2278 × 1134: [Jinko installation manual](https://www.jinkosolar.com/uploads/TUV%20DV%20Manual%EF%BC%882021-11-12%29.pdf).

Custom dimensions support other products. No product images are included.
