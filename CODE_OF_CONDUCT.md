# Code of conduct

HomeTwin is a friendly community project. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**We expect** respect, patience with newcomers, constructive feedback on the work (not the person), and honesty about what was tested.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing other people's private data (including photos or plans of someone else's home), and spam.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments and may block participants who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer via the GitHub profile [@knaackee](https://github.com/knaackee).
